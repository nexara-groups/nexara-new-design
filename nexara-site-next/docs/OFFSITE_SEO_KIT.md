## Do these in order

### Week 1: the essentials
1. **Google Business Profile**: confirm name, address, phone and website match the identity block. Add all four categories, the services list, the long description, office and team photos, and opening hours. Post once a week.
2. **Google Search Console**: add `nexaragroups.com` as a domain property, submit `https://nexaragroups.com/sitemap.xml`, then use URL Inspection to request indexing for `/`, `/neo/company`, `/neo/labs`, `/neo/marketing`, `/neo/academy` and `/blog/what-is-nexara`.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: import the site from Search Console and submit the same sitemap. Bing feeds ChatGPT and Copilot answers.
4. **Reviews**: ask each live client (Sai Nirmaan Architects, Sri Engineering Works, Happy Farms, Rise Medical Hub, Qualigene) for a Google review. Send them the review link from the Business Profile. Reply to every review.

### Week 2: profiles that rank for the brand name
Create or complete each one with the identity block and the short description. These pages often rank on page one for a company name, which pushes unrelated "Nexara" results down.

After each profile is live, send the URL so it can be added to `DATA.contact.social` in `src/lib/data.ts`. The site then publishes it as `sameAs`, which tells Google the profile and the website are one entity.

### Weeks 3 to 6: links and authority
5. **Client credit links**: ask each client to add "Website by Nexara" in their site footer, linked to `https://nexaragroups.com`. Five relevant local links are worth more than any directory.
6. **Founder profiles**: each director adds "Nexara Private Limited" as current employer on LinkedIn, linked to the company page. Send the profile URLs to add to `DATA.company.founders`.
7. **Local press and listings**: a short launch note to Vizag business media, the local startup and IT association listings, and college pages for Academy partnerships.
8. **Wikidata**: create an item for Nexara Private Limited once two independent sources exist (a press article and the MCA record). This feeds the Google knowledge panel.

### Ongoing
- Two articles a month on the blog, each answering one real local search.
- One Google Business post a week.
- A steady flow of reviews, never a burst.
