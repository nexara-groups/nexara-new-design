How we use cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar storage. Essential storage is always on because the site can’t work without it. Analytics cookies are set only after you opt in. Until you accept, Google Analytics runs in a consent-denied state and sets no _ga cookies (Google Consent Mode v2).

Managing your choice

You can change or withdraw consent at any time — it’s as easy as giving it. Rejecting analytics deletes any existing Google Analytics cookies.

You can also clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.
